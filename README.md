## 🧐 About Me

I'm a dedicated and curious Computer Science graduate with a strong passion for technology and programming. I recently completed my **Bachelor's degree in Computer Science – Design and Development of IT Systems** at Østfold University College. 🎓

When I'm not deep in code, I work as a **sales advisor at Elkjøp**, where I help people find the right technology while sharpening my communication skills.

In my spare time, I'm expanding my skills in **Vue** and **Quasar**, and I love staying social — whether that's attending events, gaming with friends, or just hanging out.

> Always open to exciting conversations — don't hesitate to reach out! 🚀
