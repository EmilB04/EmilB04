## 🧐 About Me

I hold a **Bachelor's degree in Computer Science – Design and Development of IT Systems** from Østfold University of Applied Sciences (HiØ) in Halden, completed spring 2026. 🎓

Next up: a **Master's degree in Applied Computer Science, specialising in Artificial Intelligence** — taking the full-stack background I already have and pointing it at machine learning and AI systems. 🤖

I believe there are many ways to reach a goal, and I enjoy exploring different approaches to a problem — combining creativity with structure until I land on something that works in practice.

Alongside my studies I've worked as a **sales advisor at Elkjøp**, where I get to use my interest in technology while sharpening how I communicate it.

Off the clock: gaming, films and series, and taking the drone or camera out around Halden.

> Always open to exciting conversations — don't hesitate to reach out! 🚀
